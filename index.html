Acknowledge Campus

A one-stop veterans campus near Houston for every branch: health, counseling, training, morale and events on one site, with a pond for fishing days.

Our mission

Basic training turned civilians into service members, step by step. Acknowledge Campus reverses that process. With the same structure, teamwork and purpose, we help veterans of every branch and era, and their families, step back into civilian life with confidence, so every veteran knows their service is acknowledged.
